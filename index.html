      Key concepts

      Diploid and haploid

      Diploid cells have two homologous chromosome sets (2n). Haploid cells have one chromosome set (n).

      Meiosis I

      Homologous chromosomes pair during prophase I and separate during anaphase I. Sister chromatids remain joined.

      Meiosis II

      Sister chromatids separate during anaphase II. DNA does not replicate between meiosis I and meiosis II.

      Different developmental timing

      Spermatogenesis proceeds continuously after puberty, whereas human oogenesis includes prolonged arrests in prophase I and metaphase II.
